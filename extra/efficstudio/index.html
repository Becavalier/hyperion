EfficLab中文 频道的视频制作工作台 · The video production studio of the EfficLab中文 channel

这是什么

EfficStudio 是频道主自用的本地工具，用来制作技术讲解视频，并把做好的视频发布到频道主自己的 B站 和 YouTube 账号。它只在频道主自己的电脑上运行，不对外提供服务，也没有其他用户。

What it is

EfficStudio is a personal tool that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It is used to produce technical explainer videos and to upload them to the owner's own Bilibili and YouTube channels. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Google / YouTube

EfficStudio asks for one Google permission only: youtube.upload, to upload the owner's videos and set their thumbnails on the owner's own channel. It does not read, change or delete anything else on the account. See the privacy policy.
